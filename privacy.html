Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

YT Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool run by Amar Rasane on his own computer to upload videos to his own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload). It also asks to read basic channel information (youtube.readonly), which it uses to confirm which channel is connected and to check upload status.

How data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. It is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos.
  • The app has no server. It does not collect, sell, share or send any personal data to third parties.
  • The app doesn't access other people's Google or YouTube accounts.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all stored data.
